Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Elinvi Content Automation is an internal application operated by Elinvi to manage and automate social media content publishing.

1. Information We Access

Depending on the services connected to the application, the application may access information required to publish and manage social media content, including account identifiers, profile information, media files, captions, and publishing status.

2. Third-Party Services

The application may integrate with third-party services including TikTok, Instagram, Google Drive, Google Sheets, and automation services such as n8n.

Information accessed through these services is used only for the purposes necessary to provide the application's intended functionality.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Manage social media content.
  • Generate and manage captions.
  • Schedule and publish approved content.
  • Track publishing status and results.
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the automation system.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with third-party services only when necessary to operate the connected integrations and perform requested actions.

5. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect credentials, access tokens, and other information used by the application.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the operation, maintenance, and legitimate business purposes of the application.

7. Revoking Access

Users may revoke the application's access through the relevant third-party platform's account settings or authorization controls.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

9. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Elinvi through the official Elinvi website.
